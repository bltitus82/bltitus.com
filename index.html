01 — Intro

Writer by trade.
Builder by habit.

Not a studio, not an agency pitch. This is the personal shelf — the projects that don’t belong on a business site.

I’m Brad — technical writer, front-end tinkerer, and the person behind Titus Digital Services. My day job is making complicated systems understandable. My side projects come from the same impulse: if a workflow annoys me enough times, I build the thing that fixes it.

FaceSaver is the latest example. I wanted a dead-simple way to save videos from my phone and have them just be there to watch later, no matter my device — no accounts, no cloud library to rent, no app begging for a subscription. So I built it, used it every day, and released it.

That’s the pattern here. I build for myself first. If it’s useful to me, there’s a decent chance it’s useful to someone else — so I clean it up and ship it.

FaceSaver

Share a video link from your phone, it downloads to an iCloud folder. One click, and it’s pushed to all your devices. FaceSaver uses an iOS Shortcut, an iCloud queue, and yt-dlp under the hood — with per-person “shelves,” a Chrome extension, and a native library app to browse it all. No account. Your files, your folder.
